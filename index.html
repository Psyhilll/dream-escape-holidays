We Live for Travel

Dream Escape Holidays was born from a simple belief — every Indian deserves to experience the extraordinary beauty of their own country and the world beyond.

Our Story

Founded by passionate travellers who spent years exploring India's most remote corners, Dream Escape Holidays exists to make those same extraordinary experiences accessible to everyone.

From the frozen landscapes of Ladakh to the misty tea gardens of Darjeeling, we design each package with obsessive attention to detail — the right stays, the right routes, the right moments.

Based in India, we understand Indian travellers — our budgets, our preferences, our love for authentic local food, and our need for a trustworthy partner on the road.
